Gemini prediction markets let people buy contracts on whether something will happen. Everything below follows from three ideas.
An event is the question. A contract is one answer to it.
“Will the Fed cut rates in January?” is the event. YES and NO are its contracts. A customer never holds “the event” — they hold one specific contract, so always ask which one.
Every winning contract pays exactly $1. Every losing one pays $0.
That is the whole payout rule. What a customer paid for the contract is what they risked; $1 per contract is what they collect if they were right.
An event is open, closed, or settled — and that decides your answer.
Open: still trading, nothing decided, no payouts. Closed: trading stopped, result not published yet, nobody paid. Settled: finished, result final, winners paid. “Where is my money?” means something different in each of the three.
A percentage is a price, not a fact.
A contract trading at 70% means people are paying 70¢ for a $1 payout. It is what traders think, not what Gemini predicts. Never quote it to a customer as the odds of something happening.
