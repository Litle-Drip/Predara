Gemini Prediction Markets Monitor

Gemini prediction markets · book quality. Every figure on this page is computed from Gemini's public Prediction Markets API (/events and /volume) — no authenticated, internal or venue-private data. Three limits are worth knowing before you quote a number from here:
  • No depth. The public feed carries top-of-book prices with no sizes, so order-book depth, depth imbalance and the thin/one-sided/skewed signals are absent rather than estimated.
  • Overround covers single-winner events only. A multi-winner market (a podium, say) has no meaningful sum-of-asks, so it is excluded from the average and the dutch-book count. The eligible-event count is shown beside the figure.
  • Averages state their own denominator. Spread is averaged over two-sided books only; a one-sided book has no spread a trader could cross.
